Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The Chill Temple is an independent music channel. Chill Temple Scheduler is a private desktop tool we use to schedule and publish our own music videos to our own social media accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

When we connect our TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool an access token and basic profile information about that account (open ID, display name and avatar). The tool uses this only to upload and publish our own videos, to check that a post went out, and to show its status to us.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect any data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Where data is kept

Access tokens and the posting schedule are stored only on our own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official TikTok, Meta and Google APIs.

Retention and removal

Tokens are kept until they expire or until we disconnect the tool. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions), after which the stored token is deleted.
